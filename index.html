SystemCraft YouTube Uploader

The SystemCraft YouTube Uploader is a private, personal-use tool owned and operated by Dell Bernadin. It uploads videos to his own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API, replacing a manual browser upload step.

What it does:

  • Uploads a finished video file to one of the owner's YouTube channels.
  • Sets the video's title, description, category, visibility, playlist, and content declarations (including the made-for-kids flag and the altered-or-synthetic content disclosure).
  • Posts a single comment on the uploaded video when one is supplied.

What it does not do:

  • It has no other users. It never uploads to anyone else's channel.
  • It collects no data from anyone, shares nothing with third parties, and shows no advertising.
  • It does not read, send, or store email, contacts, or any other personal data.

Authorization: the owner grants access once from his own device using Google's sign-in. The tool stores only the authorization token needed to upload, on the owner's own server, and the owner can revoke access at any time from his Google Account settings under Security → Third-party access.
