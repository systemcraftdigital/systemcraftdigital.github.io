Privacy Policy — SystemCraft YouTube Uploader

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

The SystemCraft YouTube Uploader ("the tool") is a private, personal-use application owned and operated by Dell Bernadin. It exists for one purpose: uploading finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users.

Data the tool accesses

With the owner's one-time authorization, the tool uses Google's OAuth 2.0 to access only the following, under the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl:

  • Uploading videos to the owner's YouTube channels, and setting their titles, descriptions, categories, visibility, playlists, and content declarations.
  • Reading back the uploaded video's status to confirm it processed and to verify its visibility setting.
  • Posting a single comment on an uploaded video when one is supplied, and recording a "like" on the owner's own videos when requested.

Data the tool stores

The tool stores only the OAuth authorization tokens needed to perform the uploads above, on the owner's own server, with restricted file permissions. It stores no other Google user data: no email content, no contacts, no watch history, no subscriber lists, and no analytics.

Data sharing

The tool shares no data with any third party. It does not sell data, does not show advertising, and does not use tracking or analytics of any kind. Uploaded videos and their titles/descriptions go only to the owner's own YouTube channels, exactly as the owner directed.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. The owner can revoke access at any time from his Google Account settings under Security → Third-party access, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens. Uploaded videos are deleted only through YouTube itself.

Children

The tool is operated solely by its adult owner. It is not directed at children and collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
